Patience Sorting (LIS)

The Card Game That Solves Longest Increasing Subsequence

Most DP approaches for LIS take O(n²), checking every previous element. But Patience Sorting deals the sequence like cards into descending piles using binary search, achieving O(n log n) time. The number of piles exactly equals the LIS length, and tracing back-pointers reconstructs the optimal sequence!

Binary Search Placement

For each incoming card, we don't scan piles one-by-one. Since the top card of every pile forms a strictly increasing sequence from left to right, we use binary search to instantly find the leftmost pile whose top card is larger or equal.

🔗

Back-Pointer Magic

Knowing the length is easy (it's just the number of piles), but what is the actual subsequence? We link each placed card to whatever card was sitting on top of the pile immediately to its left at the time of placement.
