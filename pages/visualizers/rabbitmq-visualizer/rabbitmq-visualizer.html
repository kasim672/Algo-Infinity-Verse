RabbitMQ Core Concepts

  • Direct: Exact key equality (`key === binding`).
  • Topic: Pattern matching where `*` matches 1 word & `#` matches 0 or more words.
  • Fanout: Ignores routing keys; sends to all queues bound to it.
  • Headers: Match header key/values (`x-match: all` or `any`).
  • DLQ: Captures messages exceeding retries, expired TTL, or rejected.
